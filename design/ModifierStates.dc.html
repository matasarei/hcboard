Modifier keys: three states, kept across layers
Idle
Ctrl
Default. Same tint as the other function keys.
Tap once to arm it.
Armed (one-shot)
Ctrl
One tap. Filled with the accent container and ringed. The chip at the top lists every armed modifier: Ctrl + Alt · next key.
Stays armed while you switch layers (?123, { }, ABC). The next non-modifier key is sent with all armed modifiers, then they release. Tapping an armed modifier again cancels it. Holding Ctrl and tapping another key with a second finger works too, like a physical keyboard.
Locked
Ctrl
Double tap, or long press. Solid accent with a lock mark. Chip reads Ctrl locked.
Every key is sent with Ctrl until you tap Ctrl again. Survives layer switches. Only leaving the text field releases it.
